KiriChess is a chess variant where the clock is a resource instead of a countdown watching you:
capturing a piece moves time onto your clock (a queen is worth 25 seconds), every move you make
costs time off your own clock by the piece you moved, giving check moves a couple of seconds from
your opponent to you, and a repeated position earns and costs nothing at all, so perpetual check
can't grind a clock to zero. The consequence that makes it more than a novelty: delivering mate
takes moves, and moves cost time, so a completely winning position can still be unaffordable to
convert - you have to budget the endgame before you cash it in. Free to play, no ads, at
[kirichess.com](https://kirichess.com).
